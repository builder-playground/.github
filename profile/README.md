# Builder Playground

**给建造者的游乐场 · 用 AI 把想法做成真实产品**

A community for people building real products with AI — coders and non-coders alike.
Ship things, share them, learn by making.

面向「用 AI 把想法做成真实产品的人」的社区。不区分科班或非科班，不区分全职或业余。

---

## Who we are / 我们是谁

Builder Playground is a community for people who build real products with AI. We don't split the room into "engineers" and "non-engineers", full-time and side-project builders — if you ship something, you belong here.

Builder Playground 是面向「用 AI 搭产品的人」的社区。我们不区分科班与非科班，也不区分全职与业余：只要你真的把东西做出来了，你就是这里的一员。

## What happens here / 在这里做什么

Publish your work, find people who care about the same problems, and get feedback you can act on.
发布自己的作品，找到同好，拿到能落地的反馈。

Three tracks / 三条内容线：

| Track / 内容线 | Description / 说明 |
| --- | --- |
| Show your build / 作品展示 | Post what you shipped — a tool, a prototype, a product. / 发布你做出来的东西：工具、原型、产品。 |
| Getting started / 上手教程 | Guides on going from an idea to a running build with AI. / 用 AI 从想法走到可运行样子的教程。 |
| Meetups / 线上 & 线下聚会 | Online sessions and in-person gatherings. / 线上分享与线下见面。 |
