## Who we are / 我们是谁

Builder Playground is a community for people who build real products with AI. We don't split the room into "engineers" and "non-engineers", full-time and side-project builders — if you ship something, you belong here.

Builder Playground 是面向「用 AI 搭产品的人」的社区。我们不区分科班与非科班，也不区分全职与业余：只要你真的把东西做出来了，你就是这里的一员。

## What happens here / 在这里做什么

Publish your work, find people who care about the same problems, and get feedback you can act on. Three tracks: show your build, getting-started guides, and online / in-person meetups.

发布自己的作品，找到同好，拿到能落地的反馈。三条内容线：作品展示、上手教程、线上 & 线下聚会。
